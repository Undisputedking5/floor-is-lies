Floor is Lies

A small, innocent square. A glowing exit. Five levels that are absolutely, definitely safe.

Controls← → or A D to move, Space / ↑ to jump. Hold jump for height. R retries, M mutes.
The ruleEvery trap is the same on every attempt. You will die. Then you will remember.
Prototype5 levels: falling floors, pop-up spikes, gravity flips, a ceiling crusher, a runaway door and one door that is not a door.
